# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We are committed to making this project a welcoming and respectful environment.

Expected behavior:
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive conduct
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue at:

**https://github.com/takeo-AAA/rn-starter-template/issues**

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action. All reports will be handled with discretion.
